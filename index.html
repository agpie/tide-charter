    Where the numbers come from

    In United States waters the chart uses NOAA CO-OPS harmonic tide predictions from the nearest official station, referenced to mean lower low water.

    Everywhere else it falls back to the Open-Meteo marine model, an 8 km global grid referenced to mean sea level. Useful for open coast, unreliable in estuaries and narrow inlets.

    Not for navigation

    Predictions ignore weather. Storm surge, barometric pressure and wind routinely shift real water levels by tens of centimetres and occasionally by metres. Use official tide tables for anything that matters.

    Sun and moon

    Sunrise, sunset, civil twilight, moonrise, moonset, phase and lunar distance are computed locally from standard low-precision astronomy, accurate to about a minute. No network needed.

    Hosting this

    This is one self-contained HTML file. Drop it in a repository, turn on GitHub Pages, done. Location and month live in the URL, so any chart you are looking at can be linked to directly.

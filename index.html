    Key templates

    Nothing on the sheet is an icon — every extra reading is a coloured dot. These are the matching key items, each a separate SVG sized to its own content with no background, so you can drop them onto the poster and place them by hand.

    Reading the sheet

    Each band is one month drawn as a single unbroken trace, about 58 tide cycles wide. High water carries its time above and height below; low water carries height above and time below, so the number nearest the wave is always a height.

    Where the numbers come from

    In United States waters, NOAA CO-OPS harmonic predictions from the nearest official station, referenced to mean lower low water. Everywhere else, tidal constituents fitted to about a year of the Open-Meteo 8 km ocean model and referenced to mean sea level.

    Not for navigation

    Predictions ignore weather. Storm surge, pressure and wind routinely shift real water levels by tens of centimetres. Use official tide tables for anything that matters.

    Printing and hosting

    The SVG carries true A1 dimensions, 594 × 841 mm, so it prints at size with no scaling. The tool itself is one self-contained HTML file: drop it in a repository and turn on GitHub Pages.
